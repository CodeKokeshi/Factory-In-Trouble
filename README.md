# Working in a Broken Factory
You are a worker in a factory that doesn't do its job properly. Inserters pick up the wrong items, drones sabotage the production line, bugs get into the conveyor belts, inserter breakdowns and the factory gets clogged up a lot. Your job is to keep the factory running by fixing the problems as they arise. You have to deal with the chaos and keep the production line moving by manually sorting items by dragging them into the correct conveyors and flicking the bugs off the belts.
